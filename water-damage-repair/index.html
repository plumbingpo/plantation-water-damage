A dry house is not yet a finished house. Even with normal moisture readings, you are left with opened walls, flooring that is gone and exposed subfloor. The repair phase is where reconstruction happens. In Plantation, we put back the materials that were lost and match them to the originals, turning a work zone into a normal room again.

The scope of repairs varies a lot. Smaller projects might involve only paint, fresh baseboard and a short section of drywall. Large jobs can include insulation, whole drywall sheets, subfloor, laminate or hardwood, cabinet boxes, doors and trim. Our aim is a like-for-like replacement, and when a true match cannot be found we say so directly and let you decide.

Dry first, then cover: that order never changes. A moisture meter has to show normal levels in the subfloor and framing before new material is installed. Building over damp wood is how mold ends up behind a freshly painted wall. Before we begin, a schedule and scope in writing tell you which items are being rebuilt and the sequence.

Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • File the written scope and photos of the completed repair with your household papers.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.
  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.
  • Ventilate with the bathroom exhaust fan while showering and afterward to protect new drywall.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.

Signs You Need Water Damage Repair in Plantation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
  • Interior doors now stick or will not latch after swelling from moisture.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • Ceiling drywall is sagging, cracked or crumbling after a leak from above.
  • The edges of your laminate or vinyl planks have puffed up, curled or pulled apart.
  • The lower part of cabinet sides or bases feels soft, looks swollen or is peeling.

Why Hire a Professional for Water Damage Repair?

Repair after water damage is not ordinary remodeling. The person rebuilding needs to know what the water did, where it went and whether the structure behind the wall is truly dry. We build from the moisture records made during drying, which tell us the studs that got wet, the subfloor that was treated and the spots needing a second look. That history keeps problems from being covered over with new drywall and fresh paint.

Matching is the other skill. If the patch stands out, it reminds you of the damage daily. We blend wall texture, feather paint to natural break points and source flooring that lines up with the existing boards where it can be found. If nothing matches, you get options from us, for example new flooring run to a doorway. Permits, where required for the work, are handled by us, and your insurer stays informed.

Our Water Damage Repair Process

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's what you can expect when you choose Plantation Restoration:

1

Confirm It Is Dry

Our crew meters the remaining drywall, the subfloor and the framing one last time. Only when each material reads normal does rebuilding start, which keeps damp spots from being sealed inside.

2

You receive a room by room list of what will be replaced, the materials we plan to use and the expected schedule. If a claim is open, we line the scope up with the adjuster.
